Welcome!

If you're here, maybe it's because you've lived through the long nights.
The silent worries.
The fierce love no one else always sees.
The days you held it together — and the ones where you didn’t.

Maybe you're not here because of a hard day. Maybe you're here to fill your heart with hope — to read stories that remind us what love looks like in real life. You're in the right place.

Welcome to SHARE Space.
This is a gentle place. A space for stories — from siblings, parents, caregivers, and autistic individuals — who have walked through both the light and the dark.

Here, we collect letters for the hard days. Letters for the moments when you need to remember you’re not alone. Letters that carry strength, truth, and hope.

You don’t have to do anything here.
Just breathe.
Read.
Maybe even write — when you're ready.

And if you leave with nothing else, leave with this:
You are seen! You are strong! You are never alone!

With love,
The SHARE Team
